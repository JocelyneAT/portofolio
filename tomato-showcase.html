The Lost Tomato: 3D Ball Adventure

"Scientifically a fruit, culinary a vegetable — a misplaced hero searching for home."

A physics-driven 3D ball platformer built around a playful identity dilemma. Guide a lost tomato across the vibrant terrains of Fruit Land, solve maze puzzles, dodge deadly spikes, and defeat dungeon bosses on an epic journey back home to Veggie Land.

Role: Lead Game Dev & Tech Artist
Engine: Unity 3D & C#
Art: Blender 3D & RigidBody Physics
Platform: PC Standalone
Watch Devlog & Walkthrough View All Game Projects

Story & Dual Identity

Tomato is trapped in Fruit Land! To return home to Veggie Land, he must navigate the locked Fruit Cave by collecting Golden Stars scattered across the world.

🍎 Fruit Land (Science) Born with seeds, Tomato has the agility to survive the high-jumping slopes.
🥦 Veggie Land (Heart) Savory and beloved, Tomato's true home and heart belong with the vegetables!

Core Mechanics & Controls

WASD Physics Ball Rolling

Realistic sphere momentum, friction slopes, and momentum transfer on uneven terrain.

Double Jump (Space × 2)

Chain jumps in mid-air to reach elevated ledges, dodge spikes, and hop across platforms.

Dash Surge (Shift)

Quick speed burst with a 5-second cooldown to speed past crumbling bridges and traps.

Level Progression & Puzzles

Stage 01

Fruit Cave Outskirts

  • ⭐ Golden Stars: Collect stars to accumulate score and unlock the gateway.
  • 🚩 Checkpoints: Respawn checkpoints ensure fluid, stress-free progression.
  • 🌲 Organic Terrains: Curved slopes requiring precise speed and angle control.
Stage 02

Dungeon Maze & Boss Fight

  • 💣 Bombs & Keys: Blast boulder barriers and collect keys to unlock locked doors.
  • ❤️ Heart Collectibles: Restore health after navigating spike and fire traps.
  • 🦇 Golden Apple Boss Surge: Grab the Golden Apple to damage and defeat the Bat boss!
