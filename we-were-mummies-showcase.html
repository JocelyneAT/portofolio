We Were Mummies: 2-Player Co-op

"Two mummies trapped in separate chambers — only verbal cooperation will break the ancient curse."

Inspired by cooperative escape experiences like We Were Here, two players must talk and guide each other through divided chambers of an ancient tomb, decyphering hieroglyphic riddles, coordinating mechanical levers, and defeating the Guardian Statue to escape.

Role: Puzzle Designer & Level Artist
Engine: Unity 3D & C++ Networking
Focus: Voice Communication & Dual UX
3D Props: Blender Egyptian Assets
Watch Gameplay Walkthrough View All Projects

Asymmetric Co-op Design

Neither player can see what the other sees! Success relies entirely on clear verbal descriptions of symbols, floor tiles, and trap sequences.

Player 1: The Observer Views the tomb blueprint, safe path tiles, and cipher decoding keys.
Player 2: The Operator Physically navigates the trap floor, turns pressure wheels, and pulls levers.

Gameplay Mechanics

Multiplayer Synchronized State

Real-time puzzle state synchronization, lever triggers, and door animations across both clients.

Dynamic Pressure Traps

Stepping on incorrect glyph tiles triggers dart traps and floor collapse mechanics.

Guardian Statue Climax

Final chamber requiring synchronized crystal alignment while dodging the boss's petrifying beam.
