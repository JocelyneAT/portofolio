Runaway Timmy: 3D Mobile Endless Runner

"Run fast, dodge Granny's broom, grab the coins, and escape through the town!"

Inspired by classic endless runners like Subway Surfers, Runaway Timmy puts players in control of a mischievous boy dashing through a vibrant European town. Escape an enraged Granny, leap over barricades, slide under market arches, and swim across canals while grabbing stackable power-ups!

Role: Lead Game Developer & Tech Artist
Platform: Mobile (Android / iOS) & PC
Engine: Unity 3D, C#, Cinemachine
Features: 3-Lane Swiping, Swimming, 5 Power-Ups
Watch Full Gameplay Video Devlog & Architecture Explanation

Core Game Highlights

Story & Goal

Timmy steals his Granny's coin jar to buy a brand new gaming console. When caught, the chase begins through the cobblestone streets!

  • Escape Granny's relentless pursuit
  • Modular European town generation
  • Climb the global high-score ladder

Swipe Controls

Responsive mobile swipe mechanics engineered for split-second reflexes and fluid momentum:

  • Swipe Up: Jump over low hurdles
  • Swipe Down: Slide under arches
  • Swipe Left/Right: Switch lanes
  • Canal Swim: Dodge river buoys

Items & Lives

Survive using stackable power-ups collected from mystery crates scattered on roads:

  • 3 Hearts: Restorable with Heal drops
  • Coin Magnet: Attracts all gold
  • Shield: Absorbs 1 fatal hit
  • Skateboard: High-speed immunity
